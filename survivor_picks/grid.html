      Reading the board

      Path survival multiplies the win probability of every pick you've locked. It falls fast — eight picks at 75% each is a coin flip overall, which is why the weakest week matters more than the average one.

      Cells show the chance that team wins, not that they cover. A 78% favorite loses roughly one week in five.
